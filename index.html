How it works

From home row to full keyboard, the right way.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or breaking bad typing habits, the path is the same — and it stays with you.

1

Anchor on the home row

Begin with F and J, the keys with the bumps. Learn where your fingers live before anything else.

2

Expand key by key

New keys are introduced one reach at a time, with color-coded finger guidance so the motion is always clear.

3

Drill until it’s instinct

Each lesson reinforces the new reach through repeated, deliberate practice until it becomes muscle memory.

4

See technique improve

Per-lesson speed and accuracy stats show your real progress — proof that the method is working.

Privacy Policy

TypeSpeed is a native macOS application for learning touch typing. This policy describes how the app handles data. It is short because the app handles almost none.

We don’t collect your data

TypeSpeed does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Specifically, the app:

  • Has no network access. The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox with no network entitlement, so it cannot connect to any server — there is no code path that sends data anywhere.
  • Contains no analytics, telemetry, trackers, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • Requires no account and no sign-in. There is nothing to register and no profile tied to you.
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, files, or browsing data.

What is stored, and where

The only data the app keeps is your course progress — which lessons you’ve completed and your speed and accuracy statistics. This is stored only on your Mac, in the app’s own sandboxed container. It never leaves your device and is never transmitted to the developer or anyone else.

  • No data is sent to any server, because the app cannot access the network.
  • No third parties receive any information from the app.
  • To erase everything the app has stored, simply delete its data from your Mac or uninstall the app. There is no server-side copy to remove, because none exists.

Crash reports

TypeSpeed does not send crash reports itself. If macOS offers to send a crash report to Apple, that is a feature of the operating system governed by your own system privacy settings, not by the app. TypeSpeed has no part in it.

Children

The app is a typing tutor suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. There is no account, no communication feature, and no data collection that would require parental consent.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app has no network access, any change here reflects how the app behaves — it cannot change how data is handled behind your back.
